        Safety

        Done right, screaming happens above your vocal folds, not on them. These rules keep it that way.

        • Pain means stop. A tickle, scratch or raw feeling means no more distortion today.
        • Warm up first, cool down after. Never scream on a cold voice.
        • Short bursts, full rests. Volume comes from support, not squeezing.
        • Skip practice when your voice is down. That means when you're sick, losing your voice, or the day after shouting.
        • See a doctor if it lasts. Hoarseness lasting more than 2 weeks, losing your voice, pain when talking, or coughing blood means a visit to an ENT / laryngologist.
        • This is a practice guide, not a teacher. A good extreme-vocals coach is worth it.

        The research behind these rules is in RESEARCH.md in the project source.
