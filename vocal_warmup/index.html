      Safety rules
      • Pain means stop. Tickle, scratch or a raw feeling means no more distortion today.
      • Always warm up first and cool down after. Never scream on a cold voice.
      • Short bursts and full rests. Volume comes from support, not squeezing.
      • Don't practise when you're sick, losing your voice, or after a night of shouting.
      • Hoarseness lasting more than 2 weeks, losing your voice, pain when talking, or coughing blood: see an ENT / laryngologist.
      • This app is a practice guide, not a teacher. A good extreme-vocals coach is worth it.
